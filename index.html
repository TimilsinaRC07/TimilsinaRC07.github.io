About me

Ramchandra Timilsina is a Senior Lecturer of Engineering Mathematics at Everest Engineering College. He teaches a broad range of courses, including Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Numerical Methods, and Engineering Calculus. His research focuses on combinatorial optimization, specifically targeting transportation network optimization, facility location-allocation models, and data-driven modeling.
