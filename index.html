About me

Ramchandra Timilsina is a Senior Lecturer of Engineering Mathematics at Everest Engineering College. His areas of focus include Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Numerical Methods, and Engineering Calculus. His research interests include Combinatorial Optimization, specifically focusing on transportation network optimization, the facility location allocation problem, data analysis, and modeling.
